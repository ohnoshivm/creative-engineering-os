One surface.
Several ways in.

Two crossing waves create this sheet. Their peaks add together, their troughs fall away. Move your pointer to bend the field; use surface tension to make the interference more pronounced.

As you scroll, the waves slip out of phase. There is no fixed path and no right end state. The movement is yours to set.

Prefer a quiet view? Pause the motion. Your system’s reduced-motion preference also keeps the surface still. The tension control works with the arrow keys.
