# Phase 3: Cold Outreach Playbooks A & B

The cold email arms race is over for solo operators.
The move is not to send more — the move is to send smarter.

**Pick 1 playbook. Run it for 90 days. Kill it if KPIs don't hit. Start the next list Monday.**
Elite operators do NOT run both against the same list.

---

## PLAYBOOK A — Slack-Orchestrated Classic

### Stack
- **Apollo** → source accounts
- **Clay** → waterfall enrichment (drops bounce rate from 2.5–4% down to under 1.5%)
- **Instantly or Smartlead** → sending with 6–8 weeks of warmup before going live
- **Replies route to #outreach** in Slack

### The Reply Loop (Roman Agent)
1. Reply arrives in #outreach
2. Roman classifies by intent: `interested` / `objection` / `not now` / `wrong-person`
3. Roman drafts your half of the response in your voice using memory layer context
4. Draft posted in-thread with the original message
5. You ship with a Slack reaction (`:white_check_mark:`) or rewrite in 3 lines

**Result**: 30 replies/week = 30 minutes of your time end-to-end.
Previously that was 4 hours of inbox triage.

### Kill Signal
If reply rate doesn't crack **3%** at 90 days — kill it. Start the next list on Monday.

---

## PLAYBOOK B — Deliverable-as-Outreach

### The Concept
Build the **finished asset** for the prospect first. Send it cold. No pitch.

The asset IS the message. Follow-up is one word. No calendar link. No friction.

### What the Asset Looks Like (examples)
- A 30-second ad cut made for their specific product
- An audit of their landing page with a rewritten version
- A sample of the exact list / report / output they'd be paying you for
- A rewritten version of their highest-traffic blog post

The asset must be something the prospect cannot accuse of being a template.
**If it could have been about any company in their category — it isn't ready.**

### Why It Works in 2026
- You cannot fake a finished thing at scale
- The prospect cannot call it a template
- It filters perfectly: people without the problem don't engage with the asset
- Volume is intrinsically low, which is the point

### Roman Agent Asset Build Workflow
Queue prospect list in #outreach. Roman:
1. Scrapes each prospect's site
2. Pulls last 30 days of their public posts
3. Reads their Product Hunt launch comments
4. Checks open job listings (reveals pain)
5. Checks their Stripe page if public (reveals traction)
6. Produces a personalized audit / sample / rewritten asset per prospect
7. Drops each into #outreach as 1 thread per prospect

**Your time on this loop**: 20 minutes next morning reviewing and hitting send.
The prospect receives a finished thing they didn't pay for.

### Kill Signal
If reply rate doesn't crack **20%** at 90 days — kill it. Start the next list on Monday.

## THE COMPARISON

| Dimension | Playbook A | Playbook B |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Volume | 20–40 prospects/week | 5–15 prospects/week |
| Time cost | Setup heavy, then 30 min/week | 20 min/day review |
| Reply rate target | 3%+ | 20%+ |
| Best for | Scalable niches, clear ICP | High-ticket, proof-of-work niches |
| Roman's role | Reply triage + drafting | Full asset production overnight |

The elite operators are not running both playbooks against the same list.
